    03 / The method

    The TAKTINO Method.

    Four stages we run on every engagement. Each one ends with something you can hold us to.

    1. e4

    Diagnose

    1. 01

      Diagnose

      We study your business, funnel, numbers and competitors to find where growth is actually leaking — not where it's easiest to post.

      What you get

      A diagnosis report: the problems that matter most, ranked by business impact.

      Why it matters

      You stop spending money on symptoms.

    2. 02

      Strategize

      We choose the few moves with the highest expected return, the channels that fit, and the metric that will prove each move worked.

      What you get

      A prioritised growth plan and a measurement framework.

      Why it matters

      Every line of your budget has a clear job.

    3. 03

      Execute

      Media, creative and conversion work launch as one coordinated play — not as separate tasks passed between teams.

      What you get

      Live campaigns, creative assets and landing-page improvements.

      Why it matters

      Your ads, content and pages push the same goal.

    4. 04

      Measure & Optimize

      We read results against the metrics we agreed on, cut what doesn't work and scale what does. Then we plan the next move.

      What you get

      Decision-ready reports: what happened, why, and what we do next.

      Why it matters

      You always know what marketing is doing for the business.
